# Risk register

The project owner owns every risk and is the only one who can accept residual risk. Agents may collect evidence and flag triggers. **High** = possible unauthorized disclosure, wrong charge, invalid transition, data loss, or blocked release. **Medium** = rework or reduced confidence that can be contained before release. Package and gate tickets are listed in the [delivery plan](delivery_plan.md#work-packages).

## Review

- Review affected risks at each Verification and before each gate, within the daily review time.
- An agent that detects a trigger stops the affected work, records evidence on the ticket, and flags the owner. The owner then picks one: correction, a new schedule, or an addendum.
- A risk is controlled only when its evidence shows the mitigation works and the owner accepts it. Reopen the risk if requirements or evidence change.
