# FixOps risk register

**Baseline:** Active SRS 1.0.1: original 1.0.0 / SPEC-GATE-01 plus human-approved [ADD-001](../spec/addenda/ADD-001-permissions.md).  
**Planning window:** 19–30 October 2026, weekdays, Europe/Kyiv. The permission clarification and planning package are approved; PLAN-GATE is GO as of 2026-10-15. Dates remain provisional forecasts subject to platform estimates and execution evidence.  
**Accountable owner:** Dmytro Shadura for every risk. Agents may collect evidence and flag triggers; they cannot accept residual risk, validate intent, or decide a Gate.

All risks below are open until their linked verification evidence is reviewed. **High** means a potential unauthorized disclosure, incorrect charge, invalid business transition, unrecoverable data, or blocked release; **Medium** means avoidable rework or reduced confidence that can be contained before release. Priority expresses consequence and management urgency, not a measured probability.

## Review and escalation

- Review this register with the plan before PLAN-GATE. Recheck affected rows during each Verification and before its Gate; during implementation, use the existing daily human review slot to inspect new triggers and the reviewer queue. This consumes the same one-hour daily capacity, not an additional allowance.
- Any agent detecting a trigger stops the affected dependent work, preserves evidence in its ticket, and flags Dmytro. Dmytro chooses correction, a revised schedule, or a requirement Addendum; no agent closes the risk through a Gate decision.
- A risk is controlled only when its linked evidence demonstrates the stated mitigation and Dmytro records acceptance. Reopen it when requirements, shared contracts, or verification evidence change. Do not relabel an unresolved acceptance failure as an accepted residual risk.
